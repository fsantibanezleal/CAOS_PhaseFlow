# PyTorch frontier training · 03 · Applying it to another product

Treat this component as a research method, not a general scheduling package. A transfer needs a new reviewed
problem definition that specifies the graph, resource vectors, objective, discount convention, eligibility
rules, action mask, state, partial-plan semantics, feature sources, splits, runtime budget and independent
validator. A task ranking learned for another objective cannot be relabeled as a discounted block-value policy.

Keep training data and checkpoints in explicitly configured external roots. Hash the frozen feature and
normalization contract with the checkpoint, seal test and product evaluation cases until model selection is
complete, and preserve failed, partial and unavailable outcomes. The interface must show the exact validated
schedule and its scope; it must not imply that the browser executes the training model.

See the research limits and no-transfer claims in the [approved SDD amendment](../../design/features/learned-frontier/SDD-amendment-0.12.000.md).
