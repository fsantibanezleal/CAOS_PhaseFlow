# State-conditioned frontier policy: requirements

Status: approved design direction with a partial vertical implementation. Felipe approved the method direction on 2026-11-01. Seeded four-family graph generation, a frozen disjoint split ledger, feasibility masking, deterministic replay, independent validation, CPU PPO mechanics, capped member receipts, hash-checked checkpoint loading, and a fail-closed CPIT adapter now have targeted tests. Runs v1 and v2 both ended without a complete validation-selected checkpoint; see [training protocol amendment 01](training-protocol-amendment-01.md) and [amendment 02](training-protocol-amendment-02.md). A fresh run under amendment 02, application replay and release evidence remain pending. The detailed specification and release boundary are in [`SDD-amendment-0.12.000.md`](SDD-amendment-0.12.000.md).

| ID | EARS requirement | Named verification gate |
|---|---|---|
| FP-001 | WHEN the policy emits a block assignment, THE block SHALL be unassigned, precedence eligible in that period, and within every residual resource capacity. | `tests/adversarial/test_frontier_features.py::test_adversarial_mask_rejects_hidden_predecessor_and_resource_overrun` |
| FP-002 | WHEN a policy schedule is exported, THE independent product validator SHALL recompute precedence, period resources, uniqueness and discounted NPV before the result enters a trace. | `tests/adversarial/test_frontier_features.py::test_adversarial_validator_rejects_precedence_reversed_in_time`; `tests/adversarial/test_frontier_features.py::test_adversarial_independent_validator_rejects_duplicate_and_overcapacity_assignments`; `tests/test_frontier_cases.py::test_product_result_recomputes_objective_and_reports_partial_coverage` |
| FP-003 | WHEN the same frozen checkpoint, inputs and settings are replayed, THE runner SHALL return the same action sequence, schedule and objective. | `tests/adversarial/test_frontier_ppo.py::test_ensemble_averages_unmasked_logits_then_applies_common_action_mask` |
| FP-004 | WHEN features are normalized, THE normalizer SHALL be fitted only on the training split, hashed with the generated training rows, embedded in every checkpoint, and SHALL reject non-finite, missing or disallowed features. | `tests/test_frontier_data.py::test_frontier_training_normalizer_uses_only_named_train_rows_and_hashes_source_bytes`; `tests/adversarial/test_frontier_features.py::test_adversarial_feature_normalizer_rejects_test_fit_and_leakage_fields`; `tests/adversarial/test_frontier_features.py::test_adversarial_feature_normalizer_rejects_nonfinite_and_undeclared_constants`; `tests/adversarial/test_frontier_ppo.py::test_training_emits_capped_run_receipt_and_checkpoint_round_trips` |
| FP-005 | WHEN training, validation, test and product cases are assigned, THEIR seeds and synthetic graph-family parameters SHALL be disjoint, and product cases SHALL remain closed until the checkpoint and settings are frozen. | `tests/adversarial/test_frontier_features.py::test_adversarial_split_manifest_rejects_seed_family_and_product_case_leakage` |
| FP-006 | WHEN a schedule is partial or the policy abstains, THE result SHALL expose scheduled tonnage/value coverage and its reason; it SHALL NOT be labeled a complete plan. | `tests/adversarial/test_frontier_ppo.py::test_adversarial_partial_schedule_reports_tonnage_value_coverage_and_stop_reason` |
| FP-007 | WHEN training reaches its declared transition or resource cap, THE runner SHALL save the final receipt and preserve negative/failed outcomes rather than silently extending the budget. | `scripts/train_frontier_policy.py --check`; `tests/adversarial/test_frontier_ppo.py::test_training_emits_capped_run_receipt_and_checkpoint_round_trips` |
| FP-008 | WHEN the method is exposed in the app, THE app SHALL replay the committed, independently validated schedule and show model provenance, coverage and limits in EN/ES. | Preliminary receipt gate: `tests/adversarial/test_frontier_release.py::test_adversarial_release_receipt_keeps_failed_cases_and_checks_partial_coverage`. The route, locale, theme and browser replay gate remains required before app exposure. |
| FP-009 | WHEN the method is baked for release, THE all-case outputs SHALL come from one product version, engine pin, frozen checkpoint and feature contract. | `scripts/evaluate_frontier_product_cases.py`; `tests/test_frontier_evaluation.py::test_baseline_reader_checks_all_thirteen_exact_version_traces_and_filters_other_objectives`; `tests/test_frontier_evaluation.py::test_baseline_pairing_requires_exact_case_instance_and_scenario`; `tests/adversarial/test_frontier_release.py::test_adversarial_release_receipt_rejects_mixed_versions_and_missing_cases`; `scripts/check_frontier_release.py` |
| FP-010 | WHEN the policy checkpoint or training data are persisted, THEY SHALL remain under the configured device model/training roots, with only permitted receipts and derived traces committed. | `tests/adversarial/test_frontier_ppo.py::test_adversarial_training_rejects_repository_checkpoint_root` |
| FP-011 | WHEN a training budget expires during validation, THE partial validation SHALL be discarded and SHALL NOT select a checkpoint or exceed the declared member cap through continued validation. | `tests/adversarial/test_frontier_ppo.py::test_budget_expiry_during_validation_does_not_select_partial_checkpoint`; `tests/adversarial/test_frontier_ppo.py::test_validation_member_returns_no_partial_report_when_budget_is_expired`. |
| FP-012 | WHEN a validated product schedule enters the replay bundle, THE evaluator SHALL derive every period row from the in-memory assignments and source CPIT instance, reconcile discounted cash flow to the independently recomputed NPV, and omit per-block assignments for licensed MineLib cases. | `tests/test_frontier_cases.py::test_product_period_aggregates_reconcile_without_exporting_block_schedule`; `scripts/check_artifacts.py`; license scan. |
| FP-013 | WHEN evaluation follows training from a later reporting commit, THE receipt SHALL distinguish the training source from the evaluation source and prove that the split, feature, policy, PPO, checkpoint-selection and training-protocol files are unchanged. | `scripts/evaluate_frontier_policy.py::_validate_frozen_method_source`; `tests/test_frontier_evaluation.py::test_frozen_training_commit_may_have_result_only_evaluation_revision`. |
