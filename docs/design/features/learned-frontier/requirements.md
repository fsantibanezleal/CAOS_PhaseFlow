# State-conditioned frontier policy: requirements

Status: approved design direction with a completed frozen evaluation and an incomplete release vertical. The five-member `20261111-bs2-ppo-v4` ensemble was evaluated once on the disjoint 500-case `test-split-recovery-02.json` sample and once across the 13 declared product cases. All synthetic schedules were feasible but partial; mean normalized NPV was 0.000847, median NPV and value coverage were zero, and mean tonnage coverage was 0.001164. The product-case result was 11 partial and 2 failed: the one-resource `ctrl-degenerate` input is unsupported, and `zuck-small-declared` lacks a source-backed grade feature. The first two test attempts remain consumed and excluded. The amended product evaluation corrects only the certified-bound field and gap percentage using the frozen baseline trace; assignments, NPV, coverage, comparisons, status and timing do not change. An external structural candidate passes the receipt and artifact checks, but the repository's committed 0.11.000 artifacts still require a complete 0.12.000 bake. App replay, final bilingual manuscripts/notebooks, independent review and deployment remain pending. See [protocol amendments 01](training-protocol-amendment-01.md), [02](training-protocol-amendment-02.md), [03](training-protocol-amendment-03-test-recovery.md), [04](training-protocol-amendment-04-test-recovery.md), [05](training-protocol-amendment-05-results.md), and [the design specification](SDD-amendment-0.12.000.md).
